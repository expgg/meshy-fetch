Meshy Fetch

Free client-side 3D model & texture exporter for Meshy.ai.
Direct memory interception. Zero paywalls. No external servers.

100% Free & Open Source • Client-Side Only
⚡
Instant GLB Interception
Sniffs raw binary glTF buffers directly from browser WebGL loader memory without downloading quotas.
🔄
Client-Side OBJ Conversion
Converts GLB models directly into standard Wavefront OBJ + MTL files locally on your machine.
🎨
Full 4K PBR Textures
Extracts BaseColor, Roughness, Metallic, and Normal maps directly from generation task outputs.
🌐
Workspace & Community Support
Seamlessly works on your personal creations as well as any publicly shared model in the Community showcase.

Why this project exists

Meshy renders generated 3D meshes directly in your browser's WebGL canvas. Previous third-party extensions paywalled these models behind remote tollbooth servers.

Meshy Fetch bypasses all remote paywalls by running 100% locally in your own browser to export high-resolution GLBs, OBJs, and PBR textures with zero telemetry or subscription locks.

Load Unpacked in Chrome / Edge / Brave • 100% Open Source
