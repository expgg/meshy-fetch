Meshy Fetch

Client-side 3D model & texture exporter for Meshy.ai.
No paywalls. No external servers. No rate limits.

100% Free & Open Source

Why this project exists

Meshy renders generated 3D meshes directly in your browser's WebGL canvas. Previous extensions paywalled these models behind external tollbooth servers.

Meshy Fetch runs client-side inside your own browser, and exports uncompressed GLB, OBJ, and PBR textures with zero tracking.
